Privacy Policy

Application: Tuan Dau Bac Auto Post · Last updated: 10 November 2026

1. Who we are

Tuan Dau Bac Auto Post is an internal tool operated by the owner of the Facebook Pages TUẤN ĐẦU BẠC, Vườn Trong Nhà and BĐS Nhà Phố Long Biên-Gia Lâm (real-estate and plant-shop Pages in Long Biên, Hà Nội, Việt Nam). The application is used solely to manage these Pages: publishing posts and stories, reading and replying to comments, and reading and replying to Messenger messages sent to the Pages.

Contact: Tuấn Đầu Bạc — 24 ngõ 82 Phú Viên, Bồ Đề, Long Biên, Hà Nội · Phone/Zalo: 0941 507 815 · Email: bds.tuandaubac@gmail.com

2. What data we process

Through the Facebook Graph API, the application processes only data belonging to the Pages listed above:

  • Page posts, stories and their engagement (comments, reactions);
  • Comments written under Page posts, including the commenter's public name and profile identifier;
  • Messenger conversations sent to the Pages, including the sender's name, page-scoped identifier (PSID) and the content of the messages.

We do not access your personal Facebook account, your friends list, or any data from Pages we do not manage.

3. How we use the data

  • To reply to frequently asked questions (prices of listed properties, opening hours, address, availability) using pre-approved reply templates;
  • To notify the Page owner about questions that need a personal answer;
  • To publish the Pages' own posts and stories on a schedule set by the owner.

We never sell personal data, never share it with third parties for marketing, and never use it to build advertising profiles.

4. Retention

Message and comment content is processed transiently to generate a reply. Operational logs that record which messages have already been answered are kept only as long as needed for the service to work and contain message identifiers, not copies of conversations for resale or analysis.

5. Data deletion requests

You may ask us to delete the personal data we hold about you (for example, your Messenger conversation with one of our Pages) at any time:

  • Send an email to bds.tuandaubac@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request” and tell us your Facebook name and which Page you contacted; or
  • Call/message 0941 507 815 with the same information.

We will delete the data within 30 days and confirm when it is done. You can also delete the conversation from your own Messenger at any time.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.

2. Dữ liệu chúng tôi xử lý

Chúng tôi không bán dữ liệu cá nhân, không chia sẻ cho bên thứ ba vì mục đích quảng cáo và không dùng dữ liệu để xây dựng hồ sơ quảng cáo.
